## Goal
Service template in `templates/service/` and `scripts/new-service.sh <name>
<language>` that generates the service directory, Dockerfile, README,
healthcheck and the Compose entry. A new service in one command.

## What Was Done
- `templates/service/`: `Dockerfile.tmpl` (stage contract `toolchain`, `lint`,
  `test`, `build`, `runtime`; OCI labels; non-root user; ports 8080/9090),
  `Makefile.tmpl` (component targets `build`, `test`, `lint`, `fmt`, `run`, all
  through Docker, per ADR-0002), `README.md.tmpl`, `dockerignore.tmpl` and
  Compose fragments for the service block, `depends_on`, embedded-database
  volume mount, database block and volume entry.
- `scripts/new-service.sh`: renders the template with placeholder
  substitution; carries the catalog of all 35 services from `CLAUDE.md`
  section 2 (language, team, database, container/embedded, purpose); validates
  the kebab-case name; warns on a language that differs from the catalog;
  requires `--team` and `--db` for services outside the catalog; inserts the
  Compose blocks above the `# AAAS:VOLUMES` marker in `compose/services.yml`
  and the volume entry after it; appends `<SERVICE>_DB_PASSWORD=change-me` to
  `.env.example`; copies `templates/docs/` when present (P0.7); runs
  `docker compose config`; prints the remaining markers and next steps.
- Memory limits by language class with a justification comment in the block
  (64m compiled, 128m interpreted/VM, 512m JVM, 256m databases).
- `compose/services.yml`: `# AAAS:VOLUMES` marker and `volumes: {}` section.
- `Makefile`: `lint-scaffold` target (part of `make lint`) fails while any
  `TODO(scaffold)` marker exists outside `templates/` and `docs/`.
- `docs/adr/0004-service-scaffolding.md`.
- README: repository map entries for `templates/service/` and `scripts/`,
  `make new-service` example.

## Verification
- `bash -n scripts/new-service.sh` — syntax ok (shellcheck arrives with P0.5).
- `make new-service NAME=successor LANG=haskell` — scaffolded with CouchDB as
  a container: service block with `depends_on: successor-db (service_healthy)`,
  `mem_limit: 64m`, networks `services` + `data`, volume `successor-data`.
- `scripts/new-service.sh digit-0 c` — SQLite embedded: no database container,
  `digit-0-data` mounted into the service, no `depends_on`.
- `scripts/new-service.sh zz-foo go` — refused ("not in the catalog; pass
  --team"); with `--team "Platform Enablement Guild (PEG)" --db Redis` — ok.
- `scripts/new-service.sh successor haskell` again — refused (already exists).
- `scripts/new-service.sh Bad_Name go` — refused (kebab-case).
- `scripts/new-service.sh carry gleam` — warning that the catalog says elixir.
- `docker compose --profile services config` with four scaffolds — rendered
  six services (two `-db`), four volumes, correct limits and networks.
- `.env.example` — four `*_DB_PASSWORD=change-me` lines appended.
- `make lint` with scaffolds present — failed on `lint-scaffold` listing every

## Decisions
- ADR-0004: one language-agnostic template with `TODO(scaffold)` markers,
  `make lint` fails while markers remain, catalog embedded in the script,
  embedded vs container databases, `AAAS:VOLUMES` marker in `services.yml`.
